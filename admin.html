Try the "POST /api/complaints" check (demo)

Paste a JSON complaint and press the button. It goes through the same validation and saving steps the live API will use. Names or codes are accepted for subsidiary, area, mine and location. Wrong combinations are rejected.

Rules checked on every incoming complaint

  • Required: complaint date, consumer, source, subsidiary, area, mine, location, site of loading, declared grade, status, priority, at least one category.
  • Area must belong to the subsidiary, mine to the area, location to the mine.
  • Dates must be real, not in the future, and in order: loading ≤ dispatch ≤ receipt ≤ complaint.
  • Percentages 0–100; GCV 0–9000 kcal/kg; quantities not negative; quantity complained ≤ quantity supplied.
  • Resolved / Closed needs a resolution date. Duplicate Complaint IDs are rejected.

Display and thresholds

The oversize threshold changes the wording shown on pages (e.g. "Oversize >100 mm"). Complaint categories are as entered; no automatic pass/fail is calculated.
